Research

Research Projects & Outcomes

Industry fundedIEEE TKDE 2023

Anomaly Detection in Complex Distributed Systems

Graph Isomorphism Networks to find and mitigate malicious software processes and gadget chains when hardware/software components of a large system are updated.

IEEE SOCC 2021

Memory Access Parallelism for Cloud FPGAs

Formalized Double Data Rate and burst-transfer optimization to cut global-memory latency on Xilinx cloud FPGAs.

Industry fundedIEEE SOCC 2020

Automating Parallelism on Cloud FPGAs

A generic template and Python-based tool for OpenCL design-space exploration on FPGAs.

Elsevier MICPRO 2020

LLVM-Based Memory Decoupling on FPGAs

An LLVM automation tool that decouples memory access from computation on Intel Stratix V FPGAs: over 2× speedup and over 40% energy reduction on Rodinia benchmarks.

Industry fundedIEEE HPEC 2019

OpenCL Pipes on Cloud FPGAs

Evaluated memory-access decoupling with OpenCL pipes on Xilinx cloud FPGA benchmark applications.

IEEE SOCC 2018

Taxonomy of Parallelism on FPGAs

A taxonomy formalizing how OpenCL temporal and spatial parallelism map onto FPGAs.
